# Product

## Register

product

## Users

VK Cricket Academy serves head coaches, assistant coaches, and players. Coaches use the platform in active operational workflows such as maintaining player records, organizing teams, entering match performances, and reviewing development. Players use it to understand their progress and performance. Public-facing academy experiences are equally important, but use the brand register on a per-task basis.

## Product Purpose

The platform runs the academy's day-to-day operations and makes player development visible. It brings team administration, match-data entry, player records, and performance tracking into one dependable system. Success means users can complete every routine operation accurately and quickly, with interactions that feel immediate and no noticeable lag.

## Brand Personality

Hard-working, resilient, and talent-focused. The experience should communicate disciplined effort, a never-give-up attitude, and confidence in every player's potential. Its voice is direct, encouraging, and grounded rather than boastful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate or interchangeable SaaS dashboards, flashy sports-betting aesthetics, and decorative interfaces that slow down routine work. The product should not feel passive, gimmicky, or more interested in spectacle than player development.

## Design Principles

1. **Keep the work moving.** Make frequent academy operations fast, direct, and responsive enough to feel immediate.
2. **Turn effort into progress.** Present performance data in ways that help coaches and players understand development and decide what to do next.
3. **Earn confidence through clarity.** Use familiar, consistent interactions and plain language so users can act without hesitation.
4. **Reflect disciplined resilience.** Express energy and ambition through purposeful choices, never through distracting decoration.
5. **Serve both sides of the academy.** Keep operational tools efficient while allowing public-facing surfaces to communicate VKCA's identity with a stronger brand register.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, color-blind-safe status communication, readable contrast, semantic structure, assistive technology, and robust mobile use. Never rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
